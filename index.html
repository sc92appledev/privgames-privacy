Privacy Policy — Priv-Games

Last updated: November 26, 2026

This policy applies only to Priv-Games. It describes privacy in the app, support requests and visits to this website.

Current version: playable offline. No saving, export or sharing of games, scores, results or any other game data.

1. Data controller

For personal-data processing whose purposes and means I determine in connection with Priv-Games, the data controller is Simone Cecere. For privacy or personal-data requests, contact: sc92.appledev@pm.me

2. App features

Priv-Games includes Tris, Four, Mine, Italian draughts, Dots & Boxes, Reversi, Naval and Memory. Mine and Memory also offer solo modes. All games and CPU decisions run locally on your device. In 1v1, both players use the same device; no internet or local network is used.

3. Data collection and sharing

The app requires no account and does not collect or transmit personal data, moves, scores or game durations to the developer or third parties. It contains no ads, analytics, tracking or third-party SDKs. No internet connection is needed to play.

4. Game data: no saving or sharing

In the current version, Priv-Games has no features for saving, exporting, synchronizing or sharing games, scores, results, moves, durations or any other game data. It does not create a history, leaderboard, backup or cloud copy of this data. Scores and results are shown only within the current game. Any future changes to these features will be reflected in an updated policy.

Boards, scores, the selected mode and the timer remain only in the running app’s memory. They are not written to files, databases or cloud services. Exiting or restarting a game discards that game’s state; closing the information screen does not. Game data is lost when the app’s process ends. The timer measures elapsed time from the start, including time in the background, and stops when the game ends. There is no archive of games or personal data held by the developer to delete.

5. Permissions and system services

The app does not request access to your location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone and does not read or write the clipboard. This policy describes Priv-Games; services independently managed by Apple, including any system diagnostics, follow your device settings and Apple’s policies.

6. Support email

If you email support, I use the information in your message to review and manage your request, reply and provide assistance. Please do not include unnecessary personal information. Messages are retained for as long as necessary to handle the request and, where applicable, for legal or rights-protection purposes.

The email provider processes technical and other data associated with its service according to its own privacy notice and its role in each processing activity. It may process data for its own purposes, separately from my handling of support requests.

7. Website hosting

When this page is visited on GitHub Pages, GitHub logs and stores the visitor’s IP address for security purposes and may process other technical data under its own privacy statement. This page does not include analytics or tracking tools. Visiting this website is separate from using Priv-Games offline and is not required to play.

GitHub General Privacy Statement

8. Legal basis and your rights

Where the GDPR applies, personal data is processed on the legal basis appropriate to the circumstances, for example to respond to a person's request or comply with legal obligations. Subject to applicable law, you may request access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, object to processing or exercise data portability rights where applicable. For processing for which I am responsible, contact the email address above.

You may also lodge a complaint with the competent supervisory authority. In Italy: Italian Data Protection Authority (Garante).

9. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated to reflect changes to the app or applicable obligations.
